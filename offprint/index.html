What Offprint will not do

Rules the studio keeps whatever you ask of it.

  • Spend before you approve. Writing the script is free; nothing is paid for until you approve it.
  • Pass a cap. Every paid call is checked against the video, day and month caps before it is sent.
  • Repaint your footage. Paper, type and colour go over the picture; the picture underneath is never redrawn.
  • Show your keys. They stay in a settings file on your computer, and the page only ever shows their last four characters.
  • Delete your work. Spare takes, removed assets and old workspaces go to a trash folder you empty yourself.
  • Use a face without consent. A photo of a real person joins the cast only after you confirm they agreed.

Questions

Where does Offprint run?

On your own computer. One program serves a page that only that computer can open. Nothing is hosted, and finished videos stay in your export folder.

What does it need?

Linux for now, with ffmpeg, Google Chrome and Claude Code installed. Footage comes from a fal key, or from your HeyGen plan for talking shots.

What does a video cost?

Only the generated footage costs money, and you see the estimate before you create the video. A 30-second video is capped at $3 unless you change the cap.

Can it show a real person?

Only with their consent: a real person's photo joins the cast once you confirm they agreed. Otherwise the cast is AI-made characters who look the same in every video.
